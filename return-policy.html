Refund & Return Policy

Transparent & fair resolution

Last Updated: October 2026

At Sardar Tiles & Marbles (Sardar Enterprises), we aim to provide quality tiles, marble, and related products that meet our customers' requirements. Since tiles and marble are often selected according to specific designs, quantities, sizes, and project requirements, our return and refund conditions are outlined below.

1. Order Confirmation

Before confirming an order, customers are encouraged to carefully verify:

  • Product name and design
  • Colour, shade, finish, and size
  • Quantity required
  • Measurements and area requirements
  • Delivery details
  • Applicable price and other charges

Once an order has been confirmed, changes or cancellations may be subject to the conditions mentioned in this policy.

2. Return Policy

Returns are generally accepted only for products that are:

  • Delivered in damaged condition
  • Incorrectly supplied compared with the confirmed order
  • Defective or materially different from the agreed product

Customers should contact us as soon as possible after receiving the goods and provide details of the issue along with photographs or other relevant evidence where requested.

Products must generally be unused, uninstalled, and in their original condition and packaging for a return or replacement to be considered.

3. Products Not Normally Eligible for Return

Due to the nature of tiles, marble, and related building materials, the following may generally not be eligible for return or exchange:

  • Products that have been installed, cut, altered, polished, or otherwise modified
  • Products damaged after delivery due to improper handling or installation
  • Products ordered or supplied specifically according to the customer's requirements
  • Products that have been used or materially altered
  • Products returned without prior approval from Sardar Tiles & Marbles

Natural variations in marble, stone, and certain tile products, including differences in colour, pattern, texture, veins, or shade, may not be considered defects.

4. Damaged Products

Customers are requested to inspect the products at the time of delivery whenever reasonably possible.

If products are visibly damaged, broken, or appear to be incorrect, please notify us promptly and provide photographs/videos and order details when requested.

We will review the issue and, where applicable, arrange a replacement or other appropriate resolution in accordance with the circumstances.

5. Wrong Product or Quantity

If you receive a product or quantity different from what was confirmed in your order, please contact us promptly.

After verification, we may arrange for the incorrect product to be replaced, the missing quantity to be supplied, or another appropriate resolution.

6. Refunds

Where a refund is approved, the refund amount and method will depend on the circumstances of the order and the payment method used.

Refunds may be processed after the returned product has been received and inspected, where applicable.

The time taken for the refunded amount to appear in your account may depend on the relevant bank, payment provider, or financial institution.

7. Order Cancellation

Cancellation requests should be made as early as possible before the order is dispatched or processed.

Once products have been dispatched, delivered, specially ordered, customized, cut, or otherwise processed according to customer requirements, cancellation may not be possible or may be subject to applicable charges.

Any advance payment or booking amount may be subject to the specific terms communicated and agreed upon at the time of order.

8. Exchange or Replacement

Where a return is approved due to damage, defect, or an incorrect product, we may, depending on availability and circumstances, provide:

  • A replacement of the affected product
  • An exchange for another product, subject to applicable price differences
  • A refund where replacement or exchange is not reasonably possible

The final resolution will depend on the nature of the issue and the availability of the product.

9. Natural Product Variations

Tiles, marble, stone, and other natural or design-based products may have variations in colour, texture, pattern, shade, or veining.

Such variations can occur naturally or due to manufacturing batches and lighting conditions and may not qualify as a defect or reason for refund or return.

Customers are encouraged to inspect samples and confirm product details before placing an order.

10. Return Process

To request a return, replacement, or refund, please contact us with:

  • Customer name
  • Order or invoice details
  • Product name
  • Description of the issue
  • Photographs/videos of the product, where applicable
  • Contact details

Our team will review the request and inform you about the next steps.

Do not return any product without first contacting and receiving instructions from Sardar Tiles & Marbles.

11. Delivery and Return Charges

Where a return or replacement is approved because of an error attributable to us, such as an incorrectly supplied product, we will determine the applicable return or replacement arrangements.

For other approved returns, any applicable transportation, handling, or other charges may be deducted from the refund or payable by the customer, as communicated before processing the return.

12. Policy Changes

We may update this Refund & Return Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business practices, products, services, or applicable requirements.

The updated version will be published on this page along with the revised Last Updated date.
